HTML - Hypertext Markup Language

Hyper: "Super-powered." It means text that isn't stuck on a page—you can click it to jump anywhere on the internet!

Text: The words and letters you read on the screen.

Markup: "Labeling." It means putting special tags around text to tell the computer what it is (like labeling something "Big Title" or "Picture").

Language: The secret code that you and the computer both understand so you can build web pages together.


HyperLink

A clickable text, image, or button that teleports you to another web page or location online.

The Web's Reason for Existing: To instantly share information and connect people across the world.
