About Georgian History

Wine:

People in Georgia started making wine in big clay pots 8,000 years ago. They are called the birthplace of wine.


King Tamar:

around 800 years ago, Queen Tamar ruled Georgia during its best years. She was so powerful they actually called her "King Tamar"


Deda ena:

Deda ena is the famous textbook created by Iakob Gogebashvili in 1876 that taught generations of Georgian children how to read and write their native language.


Georgian Food:

Georgian food is known for its unique flavors and ingredients. Some popular dishes include Khachapuri (cheese-filled bread) and Khinkali (dumplings).
